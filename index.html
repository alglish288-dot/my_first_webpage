The War for Your Attention

Every day, millions of people spend hours scrolling through their phones without realizing how valuable their attention has become.

The Attention Economy

In the digital world, attention is more than just time. It has become a valuable resource that companies compete to capture.

Social media platforms use videos, notifications, and personalized content to keep people engaged for as long as possible.

Why Is Your Attention So Valuable?

The more time people spend on a platform, the more opportunities companies have to show advertisements and collect data.

Understanding these techniques can help us make better decisions and take control of our digital lives.
